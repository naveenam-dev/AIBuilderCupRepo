**Type:** Bug  **Priority:** High  **Status:** In Progress  **Component:** ledger-service

When `ledger_async_writes` is enabled under peak load, the ledger write queue consumer falls behind and ledger-service p99 latency exceeds 2s. payments-api times out (2s client timeout) and api-gateway surfaces 502s to merchants.

**Workaround:** disable flag `ledger_async_writes` in prod and notify the Ledger on-call.
**Fix:** batch consumer writes (PR #4412), target release ledger-service 5.8.
