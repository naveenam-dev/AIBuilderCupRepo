# Customer communications during incidents

- The status page is updated by the Incident Commander or Support lead, never by individual engineers.
- Sev1: status page update within 20 minutes of declaration, then every 30 minutes.
- Sev2 with merchant-visible errors: status page "degraded performance" if impact lasts over 30 minutes.
- Never share root-cause details externally before the postmortem is approved.
